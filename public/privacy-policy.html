Privacy Policy

KJ Fitness · Last updated 30 July 2026

KJ Fitness ("the App") is a personal training app connecting clients with their coach, Kirsten. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Who we are

The App is operated by KJ Fitness, developed by Pappo Studios. Contact us at kjfitness.info@gmail.com with any privacy question or request.

Information we collect

  • Account info — name, email address, phone number, and (for Google sign-in) your Google profile.
  • Health & fitness data — workout logs, personal records, body measurements, and pre-activity health screening (PAR-Q) answers and medical notes you or your coach submit.
  • Scheduling data — session bookings, availability, and payment status (we do not process or store card numbers — payment itself happens outside the app).
  • Messages — chat messages exchanged with your coach inside the App.
  • Device data — a push-notification token, used only to deliver reminders and messages to your device.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to operate the coaching service: to schedule and run sessions, track your training and health progress, let you and your coach message each other, and send you reminders you've opted into. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your data to anyone.

Who can see your data

Your coach can see your profile, health information, bookings, and messages, as needed to coach you. Other clients cannot see your data. We do not share your information with third parties, except the infrastructure providers below who process it on our behalf.

Where your data is stored

The App is built on Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging). Your data is stored and processed on Google Cloud infrastructure under Google's own security and privacy safeguards.

Your rights & deleting your data

You can review and correct your profile information at any time from the App's Profile screen. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data — bookings, workout logs, assessments, messages — at any time from Profile → Delete My Account. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the App installed, email kjfitness.info@gmail.com from your account's email address and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active, so your coach can track your progress over time. If you delete your account, your data is removed as described above.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where required, ask you to re-accept the current terms inside the App.

Contact

Questions, concerns, or data requests: kjfitness.info@gmail.com
